# XXD Panel 014 · 当代折纸雕塑拼贴

Turn each supplied photograph into one finished contemporary folded-paper sculpture and paper-cut collage. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must retain the same source's identity, silhouette, pose, and narrative relation while rebuilding it through folds, facets, layers, nesting, cut edges, and joined paper pieces. Composition follows the source's centre of gravity, contour flow, mass, and action direction rather than a fixed centre, while visible fibre, creases, thickness, and natural shadow keep the result unmistakably handmade.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; an explicitly confirmed whole-canvas ratio or exact resolution before generation; single complete-canvas raster generation by default; high-fidelity source reference in paired modes; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; deterministic composition as fallback only; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections refine aesthetics and copy but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes target a visually equal 50/50 relationship within one coherent generated canvas. Minor generative deviation is acceptable unless the user explicitly requires pixel-exact halves; exact deterministic geometry belongs to the documented fallback. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- The whole final canvas ratio or exact pixels must be explicitly resolved before generation. Offer the archived/original-prompt 3:4, source aspect as an explicit choice, common ratios, or custom ratio/pixels; never infer explicitly selected final-canvas dimensions silently. Exact pixels take precedence over an explicitly chosen ratio.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve source identity through at least three specific cues while using folds, cut facets, layered planes, nesting, overlap, and paper-piece joining to build a single dominant sculpture. Auxiliary paper shapes support balance, direction, depth, or scale and never become a second focal subject. It is not literal photo tracing, cartoon origami, a children's craft, smooth low-poly CG, plastic 3D, symmetrical decoration, ecommerce mockup, or unrelated paper object.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 014 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve at least three identity, silhouette, pose, action, structure, scale, negative-shape, or relation cues → read the source's visual centre of gravity, contour flow, mass ratio, and action direction → simplify detail into folded planes, cut facets, nested layers, occlusion, and joined paper pieces → organise one dominant paper sculpture through primary/secondary hierarchy, scale contrast, axis relation, triangular stability, diagonal tension, foreground/background overlap, and positive/negative space → derive a coordinated vivid paper palette from the source using value steps, neighbouring hues, and only a small contrast → keep generous pale negative space and only supportive abstract paper pieces → reveal fibre, crease, cut edge, thickness, and natural shadow under soft diffuse light → make one short readable folded-paper title participate in the sculpture**.

The paper construction comes from the source's actual silhouette, pose, action, mass, direction, opening, overlap, relation, and symbol—not from attaching generic cranes, flowers, geometric bursts, or folded-paper ornaments. Contemporary paper sculpture, paper-cut collage, book arts, tactile editorial still life, and precise publication design are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the sculpture silhouette, fold map, weight distribution, palette, auxiliary supports, or folded title relation. Also reject literal miniature replicas, overly complex stacking, average symmetry, fixed central placement, smooth CGI, shiny plastic, metal, clay, foam board, cheap card, fake bevels, harsh product lighting, cartoon craft, and templated ecommerce display.

## 014 visual system

### Source identity through paper construction

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across silhouette, pose/orientation, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, mass, scale, relation distance, or dominant direction.
- Translate identity into a fold map: which contour becomes a cut edge, which volume becomes a folded plane, which overlap becomes layered paper, which opening becomes negative space, and which relation becomes nesting or occlusion.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific horizon, terrain, or spatial relation.
- Preserve recognition at a glance without recreating every photographic detail. Every facet and layer must clarify identity, action, mass, or relation.

### Source-led editorial composition

- Read the source's visual centre of gravity, contour trajectory, mass ratio, and movement. Place the sculpture accordingly; it may be off-centre, locally cropped, extended toward one edge, or suspended, but never defaults to mechanical centring.
- Use primary/secondary hierarchy, scale contrast, axis relationships, triangular stability, diagonal tension, foreground/background overlap, and positive/negative space to organise one focal sculpture.
- Preserve generous pale negative space as breathing room. Auxiliary paper shards, tabs, arcs, or planes may support weight, direction, or depth but cannot create a competing subject.
- Recompose for each aspect ratio rather than simply centring or cropping the same sculpture. Keep essential folded title, identity cues, and action direction inside safe regions.

### Fold, cut, layer, and nest

- Use folded planes and cut facets to simplify volume; layered, interlocked, nested, and overlapped pieces establish spatial depth and relation.
- Let creases change direction at source-earned joints. Cut edges reveal paper thickness; joints feel physically plausible; planes cast soft contact shadows on neighbouring layers.
- Mix larger structural planes with a limited number of smaller accents. Avoid a confetti of facets, impossible floating pieces, excessive accordion folds, and detail that erases the silhouette.
- Maintain sculptural volume without photoreal replication. It should look built by a skilled paper artist, not exported from a low-poly modeler.

### Source-derived paper palette and material

- Extract the source's most recognisable and vital colours, then convert them into a coordinated, vivid paper group using same-hue value steps, neighbouring hues, and only a small contrasting accent when source-supported.
- Use a pale ivory, light grey, soft pastel, or source-compatible light ground with generous negative space and only restrained paper texture or abstract geometry.
- Illuminate with soft diffuse light that reveals fibre, fold creases, cut edges, thickness, layer separation, and natural shadows. Keep the result clean, real, and refined.
- Reject muddy ageing, uncontrolled rainbow colour, flat single-value planes, glossy lamination, plastic highlights, smooth CG normals, metallic reflection, hard product spotlight, and cheap card-stock appearance.

## Copy and typography belong to 014

014 text is itself a readable folded-paper structure inside the sculpture—not a detached digital headline, printed sticker, extruded plastic word, or generic caption block.

- Automatic copy derives one short source-specific word or title from identity, theme, action, emotion, relation, or supported symbolism, plus only a very small number of factual or compositional micro-notes when useful.
- Dates, places, provenance, and factual numbers must be supplied or reliably established. Abstract index numbers must visibly read as compositional indexes rather than facts.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 014 construction:

- **Role:** a spatial paper component that helps brace, wrap, extend, or counterweight the sculpture while remaining clearly readable.
- **Voice:** concise, tactile, poised, contemporary, and source-specific.
- **Construction:** one short main word or title built from folded or layered paper, plus very few small printed or cut-paper notes for balance.
- **Placement:** choose a source-earned relation: arch around the form, extend along an axis, hug a contour, support above or below, interweave through layers, overlap in depth, or suspend in negative space.
- **Material:** folded, cut, layered, or lightly printed matte paper consistent with the sculpture; reveal real crease, edge, thickness, and soft shadow without becoming chunky foam lettering.
- **Script:** use a native paper-construction equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin proportions, joining, direction, and legibility rather than forcing Latin block-letter folds onto every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.
